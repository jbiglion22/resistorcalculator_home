PhoneCheck Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 9, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled in this application (the "App").

Summary
  • We do not ask you to create an account and do not collect your name, email, or phone number.
  • Results of device checks (sensors, touch, sound, battery, camera, network) are processed only on your device and are not sent to us.
  • The App shows ads through Google AdMob, which may collect device identifiers and usage data as described below.

1. Information processed on your device

To check your phone's features, the App reads data from the device's hardware and system. This data is used only to display results on your screen. It is not stored on our servers, and we do not have access to it.

FeatureData / PermissionPurpose
Sensor checkAccelerometer, gyroscope, proximity, light and other sensor valuesShow whether sensors work
Screen & touch checkTouch coordinatesTest the touch screen and display
Sound & vibration checkSpeaker, vibration, microphone (RECORD_AUDIO)Test audio output and input. Audio is not recorded to a file or sent anywhere.
Battery & system checkBattery level, temperature, device model, OS versionShow device status
Camera & flashlight checkCamera (CAMERA)Show camera preview and turn on the flashlight. Photos and videos are not saved or sent.
Network checkNetwork state, Wi-Fi / mobile connection infoShow connection status

You can deny or revoke any permission at any time in your device settings. Only the related check will stop working.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App displays banner ads provided by Google AdMob. Google and its partners may collect and use the following information to serve and measure ads:

  • Advertising ID (Android Advertising ID)
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it
  • Device information (model, OS version, language)
  • Ad interaction data (impressions, clicks)

For more information, see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and Google Privacy Policy.

You can reset your Advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads in your device's Settings > Google > Ads (or Settings > Privacy > Ads).

3. Users in the EEA, UK and Switzerland

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the App asks for your consent through Google's User Messaging Platform before ads are personalized. You can change your choice at any time from Settings > Privacy options in the App.

The legal basis for ad-related processing is your consent (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a)). You have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to the processing of your data, and to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

4. Data retention and sharing

We do not store personal data on our own servers. Data collected by Google AdMob is retained according to Google's policies. We do not sell or share personal data with any third party other than Google as described above.

5. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

6. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy. Changes will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
