Resistor Calculator Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 19, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled in the Resistor Calculator app (the "App").

Summary
  • We do not ask you to create an account and do not collect your name, email, or phone number.
  • Everything you enter or save in the App (your resistors, quantities, storage locations, history and settings) stays on your device. It is not sent to us.
  • The App does not ask for any permission such as camera, microphone, location or contacts.
  • The App shows ads through Google AdMob, which may collect device identifiers and usage data as described below.

1. Information stored on your device

The App works on your device. The information below is kept in the App's private storage on your device. We do not have access to it and it is not stored on our servers.

FeatureInformationPurpose
My ResistorsResistance values, quantities and storage locations that you enterShow, search and edit your parts, and find combinations that use them
HistoryColor band calculations and combinations that you choose to save, and whether the parts were deducted from your stockLet you review them later
SettingsYour language choice and whether the intro screen was shownRemember your preferences
Calculators (color bands, SMD code, series / parallel, LED resistor, Ohm's law)Numbers and codes that you typeCalculate results on your device. They are not stored unless you save a color band calculation to History.

You can edit or delete your resistors in My Resistors, and delete saved records in History or in Settings > Data > Delete all history. Uninstalling the App or clearing its data on your device removes all of this information.

If you use Android's device backup, your device may back up the App's data to your Google account. This is handled by your device and Google, not by us.

2. Permissions

The App does not request any permission that needs your approval, such as camera, microphone, location, contacts or photos. To show ads, the advertising SDK uses internet access, network state and the advertising ID.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App displays banner and native ads provided by Google AdMob (on the home screen, on the calculator tool screens and on the exit screen). We set ads to the "G" content rating (suitable for all audiences). Google and its partners may collect and use the following information to serve and measure ads:

  • Advertising ID (Android Advertising ID)
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it
  • Device information (model, OS version, language)
  • Ad interaction data (impressions, clicks)

For more information, see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and Google Privacy Policy.

You can reset your Advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads in your device's Settings > Google > Ads (or Settings > Privacy > Ads).

4. Users in the EEA, UK and Switzerland

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the App asks for your consent through Google's User Messaging Platform before ads are personalized. You can change your choice at any time from Settings > Privacy options in the App.

The legal basis for ad-related processing is your consent (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a)). You have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to the processing of your data, and to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

5. Data retention and sharing

We do not store personal data on our own servers. Information stored on your device stays there until you delete it, uninstall the App or clear its data. Data collected by Google AdMob is retained according to Google's policies. We do not sell personal data, and we do not share it with any third party other than Google as described above.

6. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy. Changes will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
